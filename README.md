# FounderLaunch

This is a project under the UTMIST Startups Department.
Our goal is to support student founders at UofT and encourage entrepreneurship amongst university students.

## Purpose
Most students have ideas or interest in entrepreneurship but they do not have the resources they need as they navigate their journey as a founder.

So, FounderLaunch will cater to two types of founders:
1. an aspiring founder who is exploring interest in entrepreneurship
   * "I want to learn about startups"
   * "I want to find a startup idea"
   * "I want to meet founders"
   * "I want to join an existing startup"
2. a current founder who is exploring ideas and wants to build a startup
   * "I want to validate an idea"
   * "I want to build an MVP"
   * "I want to market/pitch my product"
   * "I want funding"
   * "I want to grow my team"
